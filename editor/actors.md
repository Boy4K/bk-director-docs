# Actors

Actors are extra characters in the scene: people at the side of the road, a crowd, someone dancing.

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-director-docs/main/assets/actor.jpg" alt="An actor"><figcaption><p>An actor</p></figcaption></figure>

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ped model** | type a model or press **Pick from list** |
| **Animation** | 63 animations in groups: poses, dances, gestures, jobs, relax and sport, action, other |
| **Weapon in hand** | pistols, SMG, rifles, shotgun, sniper, bat, knife |
| **Heading** | where the actor faces |
| **Start / End** | when the actor is in the scene |

**Custom** animation lets you type any dictionary and animation name.

## Pick from list

**Pick from list** opens a grid with a photo of every ped (1100+). No need to copy names from a website.

* Search by model name, or by word in English or Italian: *police*, *doctor*, *beach*, *girl*, *mechanic*, *polizia*, *spiaggia*…
* Filter by category: pedestrians (men / women), jobs and uniforms, gangs, story characters, multiplayer, animals.
* Click a photo and the model is set.

{% hint style="info" %}
The photos load from the FiveM documentation site, so the game needs internet access. A ped without a photo shows a **?** but works the same.
{% endhint %}

## Place an actor

Press **Place actor**. Fly the camera: the actor follows the crosshair, the **mouse wheel** rotates it, left click confirms, right click cancels. The camera then returns where it was and **keyframes are not touched**.
