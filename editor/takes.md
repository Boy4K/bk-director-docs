# Takes

A take is a recording of a vehicle or a character: position, rotation, speed, steering and engine, sampled 20 times per second. In the editor the take is played back by a copy of your car or character, so you can film it from any angle.

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-director-docs/main/assets/takes.jpg" alt="The Takes tab"><figcaption><p>The Takes tab</p></figcaption></figure>

## Record

1. Go to **Takes**.
2. Press **Record from start** (or **Record from playhead** to start later in the timeline).
3. Drive or walk. A red bar shows the time left.
4. Press **X** to stop. You go back to the start of the take and the camera is placed behind it.

{% hint style="success" %}
Takes you already recorded play along while you record a new one. Record a car, then a second car following it: the chase is already in sync.
{% endhint %}

## Vehicle for takes

Type a model (`sultan`, `elegy`, `police3`) and press **Spawn**. The vehicle appears where the camera looks with you behind the wheel. It is deleted when you close the editor. Admins can turn spawning off or block models from the [Settings panel](../getting-started/settings.md).

## Take settings

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-director-docs/main/assets/take-driver.jpg" alt="A vehicle take with a custom driver"><figcaption><p>A vehicle take with a custom driver</p></figcaption></figure>

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | name shown on the timeline |
| **Starts at** | move the take along the timeline |
| **Hidden** | the take doesn't appear in the clip |
| **Who drives** | **Me** (as recorded) · **Another character** · **Nobody** (empty car) |
| **Ped model** | the driver when you pick another character. **Pick from list** opens the photo grid, or use the quick buttons (young man, biker, cop, SWAT…) |

For a character take the same menu is called **Who is it**: you or another character.

{% hint style="info" %}
If the model doesn't exist, your own character is used.
{% endhint %}
