# Texts

Add titles over the image (2D) or labels in the world (3D) from the **Texts** tab.

## 2D texts

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Font** | Poppins, Bebas Neue, Anton, Montserrat, Oswald, Playfair Display, Permanent Marker, Orbitron, Rajdhani, Pacifico, Space Grotesk |
| **Style** | size, weight, color, letter spacing, alignment, outline, glow, shadow, background box |
| **Position** | horizontal and vertical, in % of the screen |
| **Animation** | in and out: Fade, Slide up, Slide from left, Zoom, Pop, Focus pull, Typewriter, None |

## 3D texts

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-director-docs/main/assets/text-3d.jpg" alt="A 3D text following a car"><figcaption><p>A 3D text following a car</p></figcaption></figure>

A 3D text lives in the world.

* **Above who**: pick a take or an actor. The text follows it for the whole clip, above the head or the car roof. **Height above the subject** raises it.
* **Nobody**: the text stays still. Press **Place in the world**, fly the camera, the text follows the crosshair, the **mouse wheel** raises it. Left click confirms.

{% hint style="info" %}
Placing a text never touches your camera keyframes. The camera goes back where it was.
{% endhint %}
