# Speed and slow motion

The **Speed** row of the timeline holds speed points. Each point sets how fast the clip runs at that moment, **from 0.05x to 2x**, and the speed blends smoothly from one point to the next.

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-director-docs/main/assets/speed.jpg" alt="A speed point"><figcaption><p>A speed point</p></figcaption></figure>

## Quick ways

In **Effects**:

* **Slow-mo here** adds a slow motion around the playhead (in, hold, out).
* **Speed up** does the same at 2x.
* **Speed point at playhead** adds a single point.

Select a point to change its speed (buttons 0.25x, 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x) and its easing.

{% hint style="info" %}
Below 1x the whole world slows down: cars, people, smoke, particles. Above 1x takes, camera and texts speed up, but ambient traffic stays at normal speed.
{% endhint %}
