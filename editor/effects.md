# Effects

Effects are clips on the **Effects** row. Drag them, stretch them from the edges, overlap them: overlapping clips stack on their own.

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-director-docs/main/assets/effects.jpg" alt="The Effects tab"><figcaption><p>The Effects tab</p></figcaption></figure>

| Effect | Settings |
| --- | --- |
| **Color filter** | 39 game filters (Filmic 1–25, black and white, saturation, contrast…), strength |
| **Fade** | color, amount, fade in and fade out |
| **Flash** | color and amount, for cuts and impacts |
| **Film grain** | amount, fade in and out |
| **Vignette** | amount, fade in and out |

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-director-docs/main/assets/effect.jpg" alt="A color filter"><figcaption><p>A color filter</p></figcaption></figure>

Every effect has a start and an end, plus **Duplicate** and **Delete**. The whole-clip grain, vignette and letterbox are in [Scene](scene.md).

{% hint style="success" %}
Everything is cleared when the editor closes: no filter, slow motion, weather or time stays on the player.
{% endhint %}
