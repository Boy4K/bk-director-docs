# BK Director

BK Director replaces the Rockstar Editor with a panel that works every time. Record your car or your character, place camera keyframes like in the Rockstar Editor, add filters, slow motion, texts and actors, then play it back on a clean screen and record it with OBS. **Every player can use it, every setting is changed from the panel.**

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-director-docs/main/assets/editor-overview.jpg" alt="The editor: tabs on the left, inspector on the right, timeline at the bottom"><figcaption><p>The editor: tabs on the left, inspector on the right, timeline at the bottom</p></figcaption></figure>

## What's inside

| Area | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Takes** | Record vehicles and characters at 20 samples per second · old takes play along while you record · spawn a vehicle for the take · custom driver |
| **Camera** | Rockstar-style keyframes · smooth paths with no bounce · 6 easings · free, look-at, follow and car-rig shots · 8 quick shots (hood, wheel, drone…) · zoom, roll, depth of field, shake |
| **Speed** | Slow motion and speed-up from 0.05x to 2x, blended between points |
| **Effects** | 39 colour filters · fades · flashes · film grain · vignette · letterbox · weather and time of day |
| **Texts** | 2D titles with 11 fonts and 8 animations · 3D texts in the world or above a player, car or actor |
| **Actors** | 1100+ peds picked from a photo grid · 63 animations and dances · weapons · placed without touching the camera |
| **Photos** | Photo mode with rule-of-thirds grid · gallery · optional upload to a Discord webhook |
| **Server** | Private routing bucket per player · position restored on close · ESX, QBCore/QBox or standalone · English / Italiano |

## Requirements

| Resource | Needed |
| --- | --- |
| OneSync | yes (private scene per player) |
| es\_extended / qb-core / qbx\_core | optional, detected on its own |
| screenshot-basic | only for photo mode |
