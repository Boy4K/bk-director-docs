# Your first clip

A two-minute walkthrough, the same way you'd work in the Rockstar Editor.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Record a take

Open the editor with `/director`, go to **Takes** and press **Record from start**. Drive or walk, then press **X**. You go back to the start of the take.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### First keyframe

Right click and fly the camera to your first shot. Press **K**: the keyframe takes that shot.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Next keyframes

Move the playhead forward on the timeline (or just press **K** again: the new keyframe goes a couple of seconds later). Fly to the next shot. The keyframe under the playhead updates while you fly.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Watch it

Press **Space**. The camera moves smoothly from one keyframe to the next.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Make it look good

Add a filter and a slow motion from **Effects**, a title from **Texts**, a few people from **Actors**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Shoot it

Start OBS, press **Shoot video** and choose **From start**. The panel disappears and the clip plays on a clean screen.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
Want the camera glued to the car? Select the keyframe and pick a quick shot such as **Behind** or **Hood**. See [Camera keyframes](../editor/camera.md).
{% endhint %}
